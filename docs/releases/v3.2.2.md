# responsive classroom 3.2.2

- Phone heartbeat gaps no longer clear the scenario voice permission or selected voice switches, or cancel a speech result in progress. Explicitly disabling phone control or voice permission still resets the switches.
- Enabling an individual scenario microphone now also starts the computer's main speech recognizer and resumes paused voice commands. The phone control page reports when the microphone or recognizer is not ready.
- Changing music, board, or other unrelated settings no longer resets the speech decoder or invalidates the current noise reading. Noise detection continues independently of scenario voice permission.
